A free survival horror game — right in your browser

Form 0-A · site briefing

The Backrooms is a first-person survival horror game that runs entirely in your web browser. Nothing to download, nothing to install, no account and no ads: press play and you are standing in the endless yellow halls. It is free, it starts in seconds, and it works on any modern computer or phone.

A run is a descent. Six floors, and you start on the first. Every one of them is generated from the seed of your run, endless in all four horizontal directions, and every one charges a different price to let you through to the next. You will get thirsty. Your torch will run down. Something will be following you. The bottom floor is the only one with no way down — it has a way out.

Level 0

the lobby · start here

Mono-yellow wallpaper, damp carpet and fluorescent hum in every direction. Somewhere out in it the wall has gone soft. Lean on it long enough and it gives.

Level 1

front parking · one down

The car park under a supermarket that was never built. The service ramp is behind a roller shutter, and the shutter only wakes up with four car alarms screaming at once.

Level 37

the poolrooms · two down

Warm, tiled, bright and endless — down here the dread is not the dark. The way on is the drain at the bottom of the deep pool, so first you find the valve and flood the floor you are standing on.

Level 7

thalassophobia · three down

Black water under a nine-metre ceiling, and you arrive by falling into it. The hatch is bolted five metres under. You get there one lungful at a time.

Level 2

pipe dreams · four down

Low maintenance tunnels, sodium light, dust and cable. The stairwell door has a keypad on it, and the four digits are written on a wall somewhere else entirely.

Level !

run for your life · bottom

The lobby again, remembered wrong and standing in water. Nothing here goes further down. Three fuses, one portal, and a fall back out into the sky over the real world.

Four entities hunt these floors

Form 0-B · entity log

None of them walks a patrol route. Each of the four has its own idea of how to reach you, and they share one instinct: close the distance while you are looking somewhere else.

01

Smiler

A smear of darkness with a face floating in it. It keeps to the unlit rooms and works up its nerve from a distance, and the grin gets wider the longer you look at it. It has no time at all for your torch.

02

Skin-Stealer

Slow, starved and wearing somebody. It shadows you from cover a corner or two behind and will not commit until it is close enough to reach you. It takes a great deal of killing.

03

Hound

Eyeless, four-legged and faster than you are. Where there is one there are usually more, and the moment one of them decides, the rest come round the sides.

04

Partygoer

It never hides. It comes at you across open floor, hat crooked and a dead balloon in one hand — and it only breaks into a run once you have turned your back.

And what you have

You start with a torch and a field receiver, and nothing else. The rest is scavenged off the floors: a metal pipe, a pipe wrench, a kitchen knife, a fire extinguisher, a pistol and the box of 9 mm it runs on. Batteries keep the torch alive. A glass bottle holds water, and thirst is what kills the players who were careful about everything else. The bag has a weight limit, so every pickup is really a decision about what you put back down.

Frequently asked questions

Form 0-C · enquiries
What are the Backrooms?
An internet horror legend about noclipping out of reality and landing in an endless off-yellow office — damp carpet, buzzing fluorescent lights, about six hundred million square miles of empty rooms, and something in there with you. This game is one playable version of it, not an official adaptation of any other Backrooms project.
Is The Backrooms free to play?
Yes, completely. There is no purchase, no subscription, no sign-up and no advertising. The source code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence.
Do I have to download or install anything?
No. It is a WebGL game and it runs in the browser tab you are reading this in. Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari all work, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.
Can I play the Backrooms on a phone?
Yes. On a touchscreen the game swaps to an on-screen rig: a stick under your left thumb, look with the right, and labelled buttons that appear only when the thing in your hand can actually use them.
How many levels are there?
Six: Level 0, Level 1, Level 37, Level 7, Level 2 and Level !. Each one is endless in every horizontal direction and built fresh from the seed of your run, so no two descents are the same maze.
How do you escape the Backrooms?
Get to the bottom floor, find the three fuses scattered across it, carry them to the exit portal and step through. You can leave with fewer than three if your nerve goes — the door still opens, just barely.
Does the game save my progress?
Pausing writes a checkpoint into your own browser, so you can close the tab and pick the run up later. Dying clears it: a checkpoint is for putting the game down, not for a second try at the same corner. Records stay local too. The only thing the game sends is an anonymous count of started and engaged runs with a country, no IP and no identifier, and ?telemetry=off switches even that off.
Can I replay the same maze?
Yes. Every run has a seed and it is in the address bar — ?seed=1234. Send someone that link and they get your maze, your fuses and your exit, exactly where you found them.
