A free survival horror game — right in your browser

The Backrooms is a first-person survival horror game that runs entirely in your web browser. There is nothing to download, nothing to install and no account to create: press play and you are inside the endless yellow halls. Free, instant, on any modern computer.

An infinite maze

Every run is procedurally generated — mono-yellow office halls, pitch-black pipe tunnels, the flooded poolrooms. No two descents are ever the same.

You are being hunted

Four entities stalk the levels. They follow out of sight, freeze when you spot them, slip behind corners — and strike the moment you look away.

Scavenge & survive

You start with a torch and a receiver. Find weapons, batteries and ammunition, carry water in a bottle, and decide when to fight and when to run.

There is a way out

Three fuses are buried in the levels. Bring them to the exit portal, step through, and fall out of the Backrooms into the sky above the real world. Leave early with fewer fuses if your nerve goes.

Sound is everything

Every noise is synthesized live: the fluorescent hum, your heartbeat, and the whispers that mean something is close. Positional 3D audio — trust your ears.

Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump · C/Ctrl crouch · Shift run

E interact / fill bottle · LMB attack · RMB block/aim/drink · TAB inventory · G drop held item · F torch · R switch receiver

1–9/0 quick equip · mouse wheel cycle items

On phones and tablets: left thumb steers, right thumb looks, buttons do the rest. Push the stick to the rim to sprint. Tap the hotbar to swap what's in your hand.
